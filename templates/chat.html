About this application

Medical Chatbot

Medical Chatbot is a retrieval-augmented AI assistant created to make general health information easier to understand. It turns medical reference content into clear, structured, patient-friendly responses with direct answers, explanations, key points, and guidance on when to seek care.

Each answer is grounded in relevant material retrieved from the application’s medical knowledge base, while conversation memory keeps follow-up questions in context. Your name and chosen avatar create a separate browser session, distinct from other users. This application is for education and general guidance only—it does not diagnose conditions, replace a clinician, or provide emergency care.
